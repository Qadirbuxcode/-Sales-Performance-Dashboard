This project analyzes one full year of retail sales data (2024) for a supermarket chain operating across 4 regions in Pakistan. The goal is to help the business understand which products, regions, and customer segments are generating the most revenue and profit — and where losses are happening due to high discounts.

Business Problem
The management team had raw sales records in Excel but could not quickly answer questions like: Which region is performing best? Which products have the highest profit margin? How does giving discounts affect our overall profit? This dashboard answers all of these questions in one place

What the Dashboard Shows
•	Total Revenue: PKR 7.46 Million for 2024
•	Total Profit: PKR 1.73 Million — overall profit margin of 23.2%
•	Best Month: December 2024 — PKR 1.39M revenue (holiday + corporate orders)
•	Best Region: South (Karachi) — 42 orders, PKR 2.46M revenue
•	Top Product: Laptop Pro 15 — PKR 1.38M revenue across 17 units
•	Discount Insight: Orders with 10%+ discount show 28% lower profit margin

Key Business Insights Found
1. Q4 (October to December) generated 37% of the full year revenue. The business should focus inventory and marketing efforts in Q4.
2. The South region (Karachi) has the highest number of orders but the North region (Lahore) has the highest average order value — meaning fewer but larger purchases.
3. Electronics is the biggest revenue category (61%) but Furniture has better profit margins per unit sold.
4. High discounts (above 10%) are hurting profit. A maximum discount cap of 8% is recommended.
